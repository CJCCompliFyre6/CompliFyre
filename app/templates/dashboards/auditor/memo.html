Prepared by: [Auditor Name], Audit Partner, XYZ Audit Firm Pvt. Ltd.

Audit Scope: RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines Compliance Audit

Information

Legal Entity Name: Anixo Bank Ltd.

Head Office Location: Mumbai, India

Key Branch Locations:

  • New Delhi Branch
  • Hyderabad
  • Bangalore Branch

Type of Organization: Scheduled Commercial Bank

Constitution: Public Limited Company

Industry: Banking and Financial Services

Organizational Category: Regulated Financial Institution

Regulatory Agencies: RBI, SEBI

Business Overview

Business Description: Anixo Bank Ltd. provides banking products and services, including retail banking, corporate banking, and digital banking solutions.

Key Business Areas:

  • Retail Banking
  • Corporate Banking
  • Digital Banking
  • Wealth Management
  • Payment Processing

Revenue: INR 45,000 Crore (FY 2023–2024)

Net Profit: INR 5,500 Crore

Total Assets: INR 5,80,000 Crore

Organizational Structure

CEO: Mr. Rahul Mehta

CISO: Ms. Neha Verma

CFO: Mr. Ajay Khurana

Head of IT: Mr. Siddharth Nair

The bank's management team includes a strong focus on technology and risk management, with dedicated teams for compliance and cybersecurity.

Regulatory Environment

Key Regulations:

  • RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines, 2016
  • RBI’s IT Risk Management Framework
  • Payment and Settlement Systems Act
  • Indian Banking Regulation Act, 1949

The bank must comply with several guidelines issued by RBI that govern information security, incident reporting, and cybersecurity risk management.

Previous Audit Findings and Compliance

Audit History: Anixo Bank has undergone regular internal and external audits in the past. Previous audit findings indicated gaps in third–party vendor management, especially around IT risk, and recommendations were made for improvements. However, these issues were promptly addressed, and follow-up audits reported significant progress.

Compliance Status: The bank is generally compliant with most regulatory guidelines, although cybersecurity remains a key area of focus. The recent digitization of banking services has necessitated an enhanced focus on security.

Key Risk Areas

Cybersecurity Risks:

  • Access Management: Risks surrounding unauthorized access to sensitive data and systems.
  • Data Privacy: Risks of data breaches impacting customer information.
  • Third–Party Risks: Exposure to risks from third–party vendors and service providers.
  • Incident Response: Challenges in timely detection and response to cybersecurity threats.

Other Risk Areas:

  • Operational Risk: Associated with day-to-day banking operations and system integrity.
  • Compliance Risk: Risk of non–compliance with evolving RBI regulations and guidelines.
  • Reputational Risk: Potential harm to brand value due to a cybersecurity incident or breach.

Audit Focus Areas

Scope of Audit: The audit will assess the bank's adherence to RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines. It will cover the bank's governance framework, risk management practices, cybersecurity policies, network security, incident management, and data protection mechanisms.

Primary Audit Focus:

  • Information security governance.
  • Incident response preparedness.
  • Network security controls (firewalls, IDS/IPS).

Conclusion

The audit will provide a comprehensive assessment of Anixo Bank’s cybersecurity framework. The key findings and recommendations will aim to strengthen controls and reduce cybersecurity risks while ensuring compliance with RBI guidelines.
