Risk Assessment Document for Anixo Bank Ltd.

Dashboard / Risk Assessment Document for Anixo Bank Ltd.

Scope of Work: RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines and RBI Prudential IRAC Norms
Prepared by: [Auditor Name], Senior Audit Partner
Date: October 11, 2024
Client: Anixo Bank Ltd.

Introduction

This risk assessment document outlines the identification, evaluation, and prioritization of risks associated with Anixo Bank Ltd., specifically regarding compliance with the RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines and RBI Prudential IRAC Norms.

Objectives

  • Identify key risks related to cybersecurity and financial asset classification and provisioning.
  • Assess the bank’s control environment in mitigating these risks.
  • Evaluate the bank’s preparedness to comply with RBI guidelines.
  • Prioritize risks based on impact and likelihood for further testing during the audit process.

RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines Compliance Risks

Risk ID Risk Name Description Cause Impact Inherent Risk Level Control Measures Residual Risk
Results:

Impact Analysis

  • Unauthorized Access (CYB-001): High impact due to potential data breaches, loss of customer trust, and reputational damage.
  • Data Privacy Breach (CYB-002): Extremely high impact, as breaches of sensitive customer data could lead to financial and legal consequences.
  • Incident Response Delay (CYB-003): High operational impact due to delayed mitigation of cyber threats.
  • Vendor Management Risks (CYB-004): Medium impact as third-party breaches may affect operations and lead to compliance issues.
  • Ransomware Attack (CYB-005): High impact because operational downtime can severely disrupt banking services.
  • Network Security Gaps (CYB-006): High impact due to the potential for undetected network intrusions.

Risk Prioritization and Control Measures

Risk ID Risk Name Description Cause Impact Inherent Risk Level Control Measures Residual Risk
Results:

Impact Analysis

  • Incorrect Loan Classification (IRAC-001): High impact due to potential misrepresentation of financial health and penalties.
  • Provisioning Shortfall (IRAC-002): Significant financial impact due to improper loss provisioning.
  • Collateral Valuation Errors (IRAC-003): Medium impact as incorrect collateral values can misstate asset security.
  • Misreporting of Restructured Loans (IRAC-004): High financial and reputational impact from misleading financial reporting.
  • Insufficient NPA Monitoring (IRAC-005): Medium impact with potential for delayed corrective actions on problem assets.
  • Regulatory Non-Compliance (IRAC-006): High impact with reputational damage and financial penalties.

Conclusion

This Audit Strategy Memorandum provides a detailed framework for conducting the audit of Anixo Bank’s compliance with RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines and Prudential IRAC Norms. The audit will focus on high-risk areas, utilizing a risk-based approach to ensure comprehensive coverage and effective evaluation of controls. Regular communication with the bank’s management will be maintained throughout the audit to ensure alignment and understanding of audit activities.
