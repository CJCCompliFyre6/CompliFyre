Objective:

Ensure that loan classifications and NPA provisioning align with RBI IRAC norms.

Test Description:

  1. Loan Sampling: Select a sample of loans and verify that they are classified in accordance with the IRAC norms (Standard, Sub-standard, Doubtful, Loss assets).
  2. Provisioning Calculation: Recalculate provisioning for sampled NPAs and compare against the bank’s reported provisions.
  3. Collateral Verification: Assess the accuracy and valuation of collateral and its impact on provisioning.
  4. Restructured Accounts: Test the classification and provisioning of restructured accounts.

Risks:

  • Misclassification Risk: Loans might be incorrectly classified to avoid higher provisioning.
  • Under-Provisioning: Bank might under-provision for NPAs, misstating the financial position.
  • Collateral Overvaluation: Overestimating collateral value to reduce provisioning.

Substantive Tests:

Test Type Control Question Evidence Expected Result
Loan Sampling Is the loan classified according to IRAC norms? Loan files, IRAC reports Loan classification aligns with IRAC norms
Provisioning Is provisioning calculated as per RBI guidelines? Provisioning worksheets, system calculations Accurate provisioning amounts
Collateral Review Is the collateral value overstated? Collateral valuation reports Collateral is fairly valued

Control Failures:

  • Loans classified as "Standard" when they should be "Sub-standard."
  • Understated provisions, increasing risk to financial stability.

Objective:

Ensure compliance with RBI’s cybersecurity guidelines, focusing on data protection, system access, and breach response.

Test Description:

  • User Access Review: Examine user access rights to critical systems.
  • Data Encryption Testing: Verify encryption of sensitive customer data.
  • Incident Response Testing: Simulate a cybersecurity incident and review the bank's response procedures.
  • Patch Management: Test whether all critical systems are up-to-date with security patches.

Risks:

  • Unauthorized Access: Inadequate access controls could result in data breaches.
  • Data Breach: Failure to protect sensitive information from external attacks.
  • Incident Response Failure: Delayed or inadequate responses to security incidents.

Substantive Tests:

Test Type Control Question Evidence Expected Result
Access Review Are user roles and permissions aligned with job responsibilities? User access logs, role definitions Access rights match roles
Encryption Testing Is sensitive customer data encrypted at rest and in transit? Data encryption policies, encryption keys Data is securely encrypted
Incident Response Does the bank respond within the required time for incidents? Incident logs, simulation results Incident response is timely and effective

Control Failures:

  • Non-compliance with data encryption policies, leading to unauthorized data access.
  • Delayed incident response could escalate risks.

Objective:

Ensure access controls are adequately designed and implemented, and only authorized personnel have access to critical systems.

Test Description:

  • Access Logs Review: Test whether system access logs show any unauthorized access.
  • Segregation of Duties Testing: Review if critical functions (e.g., payment approvals) are performed by independent staff.
  • Role Review: Check for any roles that have unnecessary or excessive privileges.

Risks:

  • Unauthorized Access: Privileged users can manipulate data or execute fraudulent transactions.
  • Lack of Segregation: Users having excessive privileges pose fraud risks.

Substantive Tests:

Test Type Control Question Evidence Expected Result
Access Log Review Are there any instances of unauthorized access? System access logs No unauthorized access instances
Segregation of Duties Are critical functions segregated to avoid conflict of interest? User role descriptions, system logs Proper segregation of duties

Control Failures:

  • Users with excessive access privileges compromising internal controls.
  • Lack of segregation of duties leading to fraud risks.

Objective:

Ensure that incident response and recovery procedures are in place and functional.

Test Description:

  • Incident Response Plan Review: Test the bank’s adherence to its incident response plan.
  • Recovery Time Objective (RTO) Testing: Validate that critical systems meet their RTO in disaster recovery scenarios.
  • Breach Response Simulation: Perform a breach response simulation and evaluate the recovery process.

Risks:

  • Delayed Incident Recovery: Failure to restore critical systems quickly leads to operational downtime.
  • Ineffective Response Plans: Unpreparedness in handling security incidents.

Substantive Tests:

Test Type Control Question Evidence Expected Result
Incident Review Is the incident response plan adhered to? Incident logs, response timelines Incident plan followed
RTO Testing Did the system meet the required recovery time? Recovery logs, simulation results RTO met for all critical systems

Control Failures:

  • Failure to meet RTO objectives, leaving systems vulnerable to downtime.
  • Ineffective incident response, escalating breach impact.

Objective:

Ensure that third-party vendors comply with the bank’s cybersecurity and operational risk requirements.

Test Description:

  • Vendor Contract Review: Assess compliance with security clauses in vendor contracts.
  • Third-Party Audit Review: Review audit reports from third-party vendors to ensure compliance with security controls.
  • Vendor Risk Assessment: Evaluate whether the bank periodically assesses vendor risks.

Risks:

  • Vendor Data Breach: Third-party vendors might not have adequate controls, leading to data breaches.
  • Operational Risks: Vendors failing to meet SLAs can disrupt services.

Substantive Tests:

Test Type Control Question Evidence Expected Result
Vendor Contract Review Do vendor contracts include necessary security clauses? Vendor contracts Contracts meet security standards
Audit Review Are third-party vendors compliant with security controls? Vendor audit reports Vendors are compliant

Control Failures:

  • Vendor contracts lacking required security clauses, leaving the bank exposed.
  • Vendors failing to meet compliance requirements.

Objective:

Ensure accuracy of financial reporting through proper reconciliation procedures.

Test Description:

  • Bank Reconciliation Review: Test the reconciliation of bank accounts with financial statements.
  • General Ledger Reconciliation: Ensure all ledger entries are backed by supporting documentation.
  • Suspense Account Review: Examine suspense accounts for unresolved items.

Risks:

  • Misstatements: Errors in reconciliations leading to misstated financial statements.
  • Unreconciled Accounts: Suspense accounts with unresolved items that indicate poor financial management.

Substantive Tests:

Test Type Control Question Evidence Expected Result
Bank Reconciliation Are all accounts reconciled to financial statements? Bank statements, GL reports Accounts are reconciled properly
Ledger Reconciliation Are all GL entries supported by proper documentation? General Ledger entries, supporting documents All entries have valid support

Control Failures:

  • Misstatements in reconciliations, affecting the accuracy of financial reporting.
  • Suspense accounts holding unreconciled or unsupported transactions.
