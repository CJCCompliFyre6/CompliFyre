Audit Program

Dashboard / Audit Program.

Objective

Create a detailed checklist of audit procedures to be performed for Anixo Bank, ensuring the program is risk-focused and covers each section of the financial statements, as well as key regulatory and compliance guidelines, including RBI’s Prudential IRAC norms and Cybersecurity guidelines.

Activities

1. Develop Specific Audit Tests for Each Section of the Financial Statements
This section outlines the audit tests to be conducted to evaluate financial reporting, assess internal controls, and ensure compliance with relevant regulations.

2. Tailor the Program to Reflect Identified Risks and Materiality Thresholds
The audit tests will be prioritized based on the bank’s risk profile, high-risk areas, and materiality levels to ensure efficient allocation of resources and time.

Detailed Audit Tests for Financial Statements

1. Cash & Cash Equivalents:

  • Perform cash count and bank reconciliations.
  • Confirm balances with third-party confirmations.
  • Review internal controls for cash handling and fraud detection.

2. Loans & Advances:

  • Test loan origination and approval process for adherence to internal policies.
  • Verify proper classification of loans (e.g., NPA, standard assets).
  • Substantive testing of loan accounts to verify balances.
  • Assess credit risk controls in place and provisioning under IRAC norms.

3. Non-Performing Assets (NPA):

  • Test classification and reporting of NPAs in line with RBI guidelines.
  • Evaluate the adequacy of provisions made for NPAs.
  • Examine the recovery efforts and provisioning adjustments made.

4. Revenue Recognition:

  • Test income recognition policies for compliance with accounting standards.
  • Review loan interest and fee income for proper recognition.
  • Reconcile actual revenue with financial statements.

5. Information Security:

  • Review IT security policies for compliance with RBI’s Cybersecurity guidelines.
  • Test access control mechanisms and segregation of duties.
  • Assess the bank’s response to cybersecurity threats, including testing of incident response procedures.

6. Investments:

  • Confirm the valuation of investments in line with market values.
  • Test for impairment of financial assets.
  • Review compliance with investment guidelines issued by regulatory bodies.

7. Regulatory Compliance:

  • Perform compliance testing for adherence to RBI regulations, particularly in loan classifications and capital adequacy.
  • Check documentation for regulatory filings and adherence to deadlines.

Risk-Based Audit Focus

The audit tests are designed to focus on areas of high inherent and control risk. Based on our risk assessment, the following sections are deemed high-risk and will receive significant audit attention:

  • Loan Portfolio (Credit Risk): Given the substantial risk associated with non-performing loans, special focus will be on loan classifications, provisioning, and recoverability.
  • Information Security: Ensuring compliance with RBI’s cybersecurity norms is essential to avoid regulatory penalties and data breaches.
  • NPA Management: Non-compliance with IRAC norms could result in significant reputational and financial risks. This will be closely evaluated.
