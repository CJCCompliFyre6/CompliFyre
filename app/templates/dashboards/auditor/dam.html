Audit Strategy Memorandum for Anixo Bank Ltd.

Dashboard / Audit Strategy Memorandum for Anixo Bank Ltd.

Subject: Audit Strategy Memorandum

Client: Anixo Bank Ltd.

Date: October 11, 2024

Prepared by: [Auditor Name], Audit Partner, XYZ Audit Firm Pvt. Ltd.

Audit Firm: XYZ Audit Firm Pvt. Ltd.

Audit Scope: RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines Compliance Audit

Purpose of the Audit Strategy

The purpose of this Audit Strategy Memorandum is to outline the key elements of the audit for Anixo Bank Ltd., focusing on compliance with the RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines and RBI Prudential IRAC Norms.

Audit Scope

The audit will cover the following key areas based on the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI):

  • Cybersecurity Compliance: Analyzing the bank’s implementation and compliance with the RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines, including access controls, incident response mechanisms, vendor management, and data privacy protocols.
  • Prudential IRAC Norms: Assessing the classification, provisioning, and monitoring of Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) in line with RBI's Prudential IRAC norms.

Key business processes in scope for this audit include:

  • Loan processing and classification
  • Collateral management
  • IT systems security
  • Incident management
  • Vendor risk management

Audit Objectives

  1. Assess Compliance: Verify that Anixo Bank is fully compliant with the RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines and Prudential IRAC norms.
  2. Evaluate Risk Management: Examine the bank’s processes for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks related to cybersecurity and financial asset management.
  3. Test Control Effectiveness: Determine the adequacy and effectiveness of key controls designed to mitigate risks in these critical areas.
  4. Report Findings and Recommendations: Provide management with a clear set of findings, identified gaps, and actionable recommendations for remediation.

Audit Risk Assessment

The initial Risk Assessment for the audit has identified several high-risk areas in cybersecurity and financial compliance, which will be the focus of the audit. The inherent risk for misclassification of assets under IRAC norms and cybersecurity breaches has been classified as high.

Key Risk Areas:

  1. Cybersecurity
    • Unauthorized access to critical systems
    • Data breaches and lack of encryption
    • Weak vendor management
    • Delayed incident response
  2. Prudential IRAC Norms
    • Incorrect classification of NPAs
    • Inadequate provisioning
    • Misreporting of restructured loans

Audit Approach

  1. Preliminary Assessment: Review of internal policies and procedures related to the scope, discussions with key stakeholders, and walkthroughs of business processes.
  2. Control Testing: Testing the design and operational effectiveness of IT security controls and loan classification processes.
  3. Substantive Testing: System access logs, incident reports, and sample loan accounts testing.
  4. Audit Sampling: Statistical sampling for high-value and high-risk transactions.
  5. Evidence Collection: Documentary evidence like access logs and financial statements.

Materiality

Materiality thresholds will be established for both cybersecurity and financial audits, based on regulatory significance and the potential impact of control failures. These thresholds will guide the depth of testing required and determine the significance of audit findings.

Reporting and Deliverables

  1. Audit Report: A comprehensive report detailing audit findings, recommendations, and management’s response.
  2. Management Letter: A formal letter addressed to senior management, summarizing high-priority risks and action plans.
  3. Audit Workpapers: Detailed documentation of audit procedures, evidence collected, and testing results.
  4. Audit Issues Log: A register of all audit findings, categorized by severity and with recommended remediation actions.
