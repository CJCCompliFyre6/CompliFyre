Final Audit Report

Dashboard / Final Audit Report

Audit Scope: As defined in prior documents, the fieldwork focuses on RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines and RBI Prudential IRAC norms, covering the identified key processes: Loan Classification and NPA Provisioning, Cybersecurity and Data Protection, Access Controls, Incident Response, and Vendor Management.

1. Purpose of Fieldwork

The fieldwork stage aims to gather sufficient and appropriate audit evidence by:

Reviewing client documentation related to critical controls and processes.

Observing client procedures to assess control design and operational effectiveness.

Conducting interviews with client personnel to understand operational workflows and responsibilities.

Executing control tests and substantive testing to validate the accuracy and completeness of records.

Objectives:

Evaluate adherence to RBI IRAC norms for Loan Classification and NPA provisioning.

Ensure compliance with RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines through testing of Data Protection, Incident Response, and Access Control mechanisms.

2. Process Overview

1. Document Review

Obtain policy documents, system reports, and internal manuals related to the processes under review.

Key Documents: Loan files, provisioning worksheets, security policies, encryption standards, user access logs, incident response plans.

2. Observations

Observe how loan classifications are assigned in real-time to verify compliance with RBI IRAC norms.

Monitor how incident response teams react to simulated cybersecurity breaches.

3. Interviews

Discuss with Risk Management teams to understand NPA provisioning strategies.

Engage with the IT Security Team to assess how they ensure compliance with cybersecurity guidelines.

4. Control Testing

Loan Classification Controls: Test if loan classifications align with IRAC guidelines through sample-based testing.

Cybersecurity Controls: Test encryption methods, access control configurations, and incident response procedures.

5. Substantive Testing

Loan Reclassification: Recalculate the provisioning for NPAs and ensure alignment with regulatory expectations.

3. Key Deliverables from Fieldwork

A. Audit Workpapers

Audit workpapers document the audit team’s testing of controls and substantive testing procedures. Key sections include:

Loan Classification and NPA Provisioning: Documentation of the sample loans tested, provisioning recalculations, and any misclassifications found.

Cybersecurity Controls: Evidence of data encryption compliance and results from incident response simulations.

B. Fieldwork Notes

Fieldwork notes capture insights from observations, interviews, and preliminary testing results:

Loan Classification Fieldwork Note: "Loan 10234 was incorrectly classified as ‘Standard’ instead of ‘Sub-standard’ as it exceeded the 90-day overdue limit, which could have led to under-provisioning."

Cybersecurity Fieldwork Note: "Incident response simulation revealed that the breach took longer than acceptable to be identified and escalated to senior management, indicating a weakness in monitoring."

Initial documentation of audit findings, including:

Loan Classification Fieldwork Note:

Loan misclassification identified due to outdated borrower financial data.

Unauthorized access to sensitive financial data by junior staff due to improper role assignment.

4. Detailed Fieldwork Steps for Each Process

1. Loan Classification and NPA Provisioning (RBI IRAC Norms)

Tests Performed:

Loan Sampling: Selected 50 loans across sectors and verified their classification based on borrower repayment history.

Provisioning Check: Recalculated provisioning amounts for sampled NPAs to ensure accuracy as per RBI guidelines.

Control Questions:

Are the loans correctly classified as per IRAC norms? (Yes/No)

Has provisioning been adequately calculated for NPAs? (Yes/No)

Results:

10% of sampled loans were misclassified, leading to incorrect provisioning amounts.

2. Data Protection and Cybersecurity Controls (RBI Cybersecurity Guidelines)

Tests Performed:

Access Log Review: Verified that user access to sensitive financial data is restricted to authorized personnel.

Encryption Testing: Confirmed that all sensitive customer data is encrypted both at rest and in transit.

Control Questions:

Is access to critical data appropriately restricted? (Yes/No)

Is customer data properly encrypted? (Yes/No)

Results:

5 instances of unauthorized access to sensitive financial data were identified due to role misassignment.

Encryption protocols were fully compliant with RBI requirements.

3. Access Control and Authorization Mechanisms

Tests Performed:

User Role Review: Assessed if users have been assigned appropriate roles and permissions based on their responsibilities.

Segregation of Duties Testing: Verified whether duties related to high-risk transactions are segregated to mitigate the risk of fraud.

Control Questions:

Are user roles aligned with job responsibilities? (Yes/No)

Are critical functions segregated? (Yes/No)

Results:

Control gaps were identified in the segregation of duties for high-value transaction approvals, increasing fraud risk.

4. Incident Response and Recovery Procedures

Tests Performed:

Incident Response Plan Testing: Simulated a cybersecurity breach and assessed the bank’s response times.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) Testing: Evaluated whether critical systems meet RTO during disaster recovery scenarios.

Control Questions:

Did the bank respond to incidents within the defined time? (Yes/No)

Did the systems recover within the required RTO? (Yes/No)

Results:

Response times exceeded acceptable thresholds, indicating gaps in the bank’s incident management process.

RTOs were met for 90% of critical systems; delays were noted in non-core systems.

Next Steps in Fieldwork:

Continue gathering evidence and documenting observations.

Review findings with the client’s management team to confirm accuracy and obtain explanations for any control deficiencies.

Begin drafting interim audit reports highlighting key issues and remediation recommendations.
