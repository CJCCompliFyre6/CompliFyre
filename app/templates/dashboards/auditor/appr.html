Approved Audit Plan

Dashboard / Approved Audit Plan

Objective

Ensure that the audit plan for Anixo Bank Ltd. is complete, risk-focused, and aligned with audit objectives. This finalized plan has been reviewed and approved by senior management and audit partners, marking the end of the planning phase and ready for execution.

Scope of the Audit:

The scope of the audit includes assessing the bank's compliance with the RBI's Prudential IRAC norms and Cybersecurity guidelines across the following processes:

  • Loan and Credit Risk Management.
  • Information Security and Data Protection.
  • Regulatory Compliance with key RBI norms.
  • Non-Performing Assets (NPA) classification and management.

Audit Objectives:

1. Evaluate Internal Controls: To ensure that adequate internal controls exist to safeguard assets, maintain data integrity, and comply with regulations.

2. Risk-Based Testing: Focus on high-risk areas, such as credit risk and cybersecurity, based on prior risk assessments.

3. Regulatory Compliance: Verify compliance with regulatory frameworks, especially focusing on IRAC norms and cybersecurity compliance under the RBI guidelines.

Key Areas of Focus

Audit Area Description Risk Level Compliance Focus
Loan Portfolio Management Assessment of loan approval, provisioning, and classification (including NPAs). High RBI Prudential IRAC norms
Information Security Review of IT systems and cybersecurity controls as per RBI Cybersecurity guidelines. High RBI Cybersecurity Norms
Non-Performing Assets (NPA) Classification, provisioning, and recovery efforts for NPAs. High RBI Prudential IRAC norms
Revenue Recognition Verification of income from loan interest and other key revenue streams. Medium Regulatory Financial Reporting

Audit Approach:

1. Risk-Based Methodology: The audit will prioritize high-risk areas such as credit risk, NPAs, and cybersecurity.

2. Substantive Testing: Detailed substantive testing will be performed on the bank's loan portfolio, NPA classification, and financial reporting.

3. Compliance Testing: Focused reviews will be conducted on the bank’s compliance with regulatory guidelines.

Materiality Thresholds:

Overall Materiality: 1% of Total Assets (e.g., INR [Amount] million)
Materiality for Loans & NPAs: 0.5% of the loan portfolio.
