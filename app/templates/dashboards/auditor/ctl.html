Inherent Risk refers to the natural level of risk in the process without any controls in place.

Control Risk evaluates the chance of controls failing to detect or mitigate risk.

Residual Risk is the level of risk remaining after the application of the controls.

Control Effectiveness Rating gives an overall assessment based on the test results and adequacy of the control.
