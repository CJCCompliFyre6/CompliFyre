Management Response

Dashboard / Management Response

Client: Anixo Bank Ltd.

Audit Scope: RBI Prudential IRAC Norms & Cybersecurity Guidelines

Audit Period: January 1, 2024 – March 31, 2024

1. Finding: Misclassification of Loans (High Risk)

Description: Several loans were misclassified, leading to incorrect provisioning and under-reporting of NPAs.

Management Response: "We acknowledge the finding and have initiated steps to automate the loan classification process..."

Timeline for Remediation: 3 months

Owner: Rajesh Nair, Credit Risk Department

2. Finding: Delayed Provisioning (Medium Risk)

Description: Provisioning for some NPAs was delayed beyond RBI-prescribed timelines.

Management Response: "We are currently implementing an automated alert system to flag overdue provisions..."

Timeline for Remediation: 2 months

Owner: Meera Joshi, Finance Department

3. Finding: Inadequate Data Encryption (High Risk)

Description: Customer data in transit was not consistently encrypted, exposing it to potential security risks.

Management Response: "We have taken note of this issue and will upgrade our existing encryption protocols..."

Timeline for Remediation: 6 months

Owner: Sandeep Rao, Information Security Department

4. Finding: Lack of Incident Response Testing (Medium Risk)

Description: The incident response plan has not been tested frequently, leading to possible unpreparedness during a cyber incident.

Management Response: "Recognizing the importance of testing, we will conduct a full incident response drill twice a year..."

Timeline for Remediation: Ongoing

Owner: Alka Sen, IT Operations Department
