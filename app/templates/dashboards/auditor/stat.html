Materiality Statement for Audit of Anixo Bank Ltd.

Dashboard / Materiality Statement for Audit of Anixo Bank Ltd.

Purpose of Materiality in Audit

Materiality serves as a critical guideline in determining the significance of financial misstatements or control weaknesses that could influence the decision-making process of stakeholders...

Basis for Setting Materiality

The determination of materiality is rooted in both quantitative and qualitative assessments...

1. Financial Materiality

For the audit of Prudential IRAC compliance, the primary financial materiality is based on the bank’s Total Assets, Revenue, and Loan Portfolios...

Benchmark: 0.5% to 2% of the bank’s total assets or revenue will be used as the quantitative benchmark...

2. Operational Materiality

For the audit of Cybersecurity controls, materiality will focus on areas that could have severe operational, legal, or reputational impacts...

Benchmark: A qualitative assessment will prioritize materiality based on the criticality of the control and risk exposure...

Quantitative Materiality Levels

Overall Materiality: Based on the total revenue of ₹10,000 Crore for Anixo Bank Ltd...

Performance Materiality: A lower threshold of ₹50 Crore...

Tolerable Misstatement: Any individual misstatement or control failure exceeding ₹25 Crore...

Qualitative Materiality Factors

In addition to the financial benchmarks, qualitative factors will influence materiality assessments in areas where potential control lapses could lead to:

Regulatory Non-Compliance: A failure to comply with key RBI regulations (both Prudential IRAC and Cybersecurity norms) will be considered highly material...
Operational Impact: Controls or misstatements that affect core banking operations, such as loan approvals, customer data protection...
Reputational Risk: Breaches that could severely damage the bank’s public standing or investor confidence...

Reassessment of Materiality

Materiality will be reassessed continuously throughout the audit. If additional risks are identified during the audit process, such as a significant increase in NPAs...

Impact on Audit Procedures

The materiality thresholds established here will directly influence the nature, timing, and extent of audit procedures, including:

Sample Sizes: Larger samples will be drawn from higher-risk areas such as loan portfolios...
Control Testing: Key controls, such as incident response mechanisms and loan classification procedures...
Substantive Testing: Financial balances related to provisioning for NPAs and classification of loan assets...

Conclusion

The application of materiality ensures that the audit focuses on areas of highest risk and potential impact...
